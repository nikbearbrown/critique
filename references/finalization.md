# Finalization commands: /assemble and /submit

For `/journals`, read `references/journals-neu.md`.

## /assemble

**Purpose:** compile all drafted sections into one manuscript.

### Compile, in this order

1. Title and authors
2. Abstract
3. Introduction
4. Methods
5. Results
6. Discussion
7. References, formatted to the journal's style
8. Acknowledgments, conflicts of interest, funding

Before delivering, mark every section that needs a person's eyes with `[NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW]` and every inference with `[ASSUMPTION: X]`. Check that the pieces agree with one another: numbers in the abstract match the results, every citation in the text is in the reference list, every figure and table is cited.

References come only from what the person supplied. Do not complete a partial reference from memory; mark it `[NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW: incomplete reference]`.

Close with: "This manuscript is assembled. Flag any sections marked [NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW] before submission."

### Silent

Assemble from what exists. Missing sections are named at the top, not written.

## /submit

**Purpose:** journal selection and a pre-submission checklist.

**Phase gate (interactive):** "Before I run journal selection, are all CRITICAL findings from the review addressed? A paper with an open CRITICAL flag will be rejected on that issue alone."

### Intake

- Field and subfield
- Paper type
- Key finding, in one sentence
- Journals already being considered, and why
- Is open access required?
- How much does turnaround matter?

### Output

Three journal recommendations, each with its rationale and with context on impact factor and rejection rate. Those figures are from memory unless you looked them up in this session: label them approximate and point to the journal's own page.

### Pre-submission checklist

- [ ] Abstract matches the manuscript
- [ ] Every figure and table is cited in the text
- [ ] Statistical reporting complete: effect sizes, confidence intervals, exact p-values
- [ ] IRB (or IACUC) approval stated
- [ ] Conflicts of interest and funding disclosed
- [ ] References formatted to the journal's style
- [ ] Word count within limits
- [ ] Cover letter drafted
- [ ] Supplementary materials complete
- [ ] CRediT author contributions documented

Where the manuscript is available, tick or cross each item from what the text shows, and say where the gap is.

### Silent

Deliver from the inputs. Flag assumptions.
